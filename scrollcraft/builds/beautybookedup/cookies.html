Cookie policy

There are no cookies on this site, so there is nothing for you to accept or reject.

What we use instead

  • Fonts are stored on this site and loaded from it.
  • Images, the calendar mark and the videos are stored on this site and loaded from it.
  • The contact form opens your email app. It does not set a cookie or save anything in your browser.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels, social widgets or embedded posts are loaded from other companies.

Why there is no cookie banner

UK law asks for consent before non-essential cookies are set. Since this site sets none, a banner would ask you to agree to something that never happens. If we add a tool that uses cookies later, we will put a consent banner in place and update this page first.

Browser storage

The page reads your motion preference so the video can be turned off for people who prefer reduced motion. That is a browser setting, not data we store.

Questions

If you find anything on this site that does load from a third party, please tell hello@beautybookedup.co.uk. See also the privacy policy.
