Questions we get asked.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your website, domain and content belong to you.

How long does it take?

Most websites are ready in 2 to 3 weeks.

Is there a contract?

Plans run for 3, 6 or 9 months. Pay monthly, or pay upfront and save 10%. After that, you can stay on a rolling monthly plan with 30 days' notice.

Does it work with Fresha, Treatwell or Booksy?

Yes. We can link your website to the booking tool you already use.

I'm not good with tech. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We set everything up for you and show you how it works in plain, simple steps.
