## Journey (beats before acts)

1. Recognition: the stylist's own morning, in their own bright workspace.
2. Tension: the phone and the admin interrupting the appointment.
3. Turn: the diary becomes the centre of the story instead of the admin.
4. Substance: the services named plainly, one cut each.
5. Range: lashes, nails, brows, aesthetics, African hair, mobile, all in view.
6. Commitment: the diary is full and there is exactly one next action.

## Feeling curve (written before the score)

| Act | Emotion | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognition | a lash artist setting up in a bright, quiet studio, cut in under a second |
| 2 | Interruption | a phone lights up beside a client, hard cut, nothing explained |
| 3 | Fatigue | an ink cut naming the second job nobody hired them for |
| 4 | Change | an iris reveals the empty diary grid and the three plain steps |
| 5 | Substance | the six real services, each with one plain line |
| 6 | Clarity | the four what-you-get promises, no decoration |
| 7 | Calm | macro nails, soft light, no copy competing |
| 8 | Confidence | auto-replies answered while the visitor is with a client |
| 9 | Appetite | short service tiles cutting across a kinetic headline |
| 10 | Inclusion | African hair braiding and textured hair in the same set |
| 11 | Relief | the inbox cut, one phone, one quiet screen |
| 12 | Weight | three plans with real bullets and Sharon's note |
| 13 | Reassurance | FAQs answered plainly in an accordion |
| 14 | Intent | a real callback form with a real email action |
| 15 | Stillness | three illustrative work frames, clearly labelled as placeholders |
| 16 | Resolve | abrupt full-bleed booking cut, diary rail complete, one CTA, Instagram-style square frames around it |

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. Act 15 is authored silence: no new diary
slot, no kinetic type, only still frames. It is the quiet in front of the peak.

## The peak

> "The little diary at the bottom kept filling up with my own services as I
> scrolled, until the whole week was full and the booking button appeared."

It lives in the fixed diary chrome across acts 14 to 16, not in a pinned act:
the rail unfurls, the last slots cascade in, it holds with the completed week,
then the final cut resolves. It has the most scroll room of any single moment on
the page even though no individual act is long.

## The tell-someone sentence

It's the site where the diary at the bottom fills itself with each beauty service
as you scroll, until the whole week is full and there is one button to book.
