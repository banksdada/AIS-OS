Privacy policy and GDPR

This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website. It is written to be read, not to be hidden behind legal language.

Who we are

Beauty Booked Up is a division of WebAwesome Consult UK. Sharon Dada is a partner at WebAwesome Consult UK. For data protection questions, email privacy@beautybookedup.co.uk. That address is the contact point for the data controller.

What this website collects

  • Enquiry details you choose to send. The call-back form asks for your name, business name, phone number, a best time to call, an optional email address, the services you want help with and an optional message. It also asks for your consent to be contacted.
  • The website does not store your details. The call-back form is delivered through FormSubmit.co to our mailbox. This site has no database, form handler or analytics.
  • Server diagnostics. Access logging is switched off on this site. The hosting platform may still record technical errors, such as a failed page request, which can include an IP address. These logs are used only to keep the site working and are not used for marketing.

Why we use your details

If you email us, we use what you sent only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and discuss the services you asked about. The lawful basis is your consent when you fill in the form, and our legitimate interest in answering a message you sent us.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no third-party trackers, fonts, videos or embedded social posts. Everything you see is served from the same site. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. More detail is on the cookie policy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share enquiry details. FormSubmit.co processes call-back form submissions and delivers them to our inbox; it keeps a submission archive for 30 days. Our hosting provider processes technical requests to serve the pages. If you become a client, the terms we agree with you will explain any further tools we use and why.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as they are useful for answering you and for any work that follows, then deleted. We may keep a small record where UK law requires it, such as basic accounting records for paid work.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, object to our use of it, or ask us to pass it to you in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@beautybookedup.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

Call-back details are sent through FormSubmit.co over HTTPS. We will never ask for card details or passwords through this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published here.
