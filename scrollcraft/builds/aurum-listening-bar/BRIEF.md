# BRIEF — Aurum Listening Bar

Delegation: User asked to “build me a cinematic website using your own discretion” and stated KIE AI API is available if needed. All creative decisions below are authored by me under that explicit delegation, not interviewed answers. No user quotations are invented.

## 1. Vibe in three to five words, plus up to three references
**Vibe:** nocturnal, intimate, analog, amber-lit, precise.  
**References (medium, not sites):** Wong Kar-wai’s *In the Mood for Love* (color and longing in interiors), Blue Note album-cover photography (cropped instruments, smoke, typography), a Japanese jazz kissa at midnight (ritual, listening as attention).

## 2. The scroll journey, section by section, in their words
1. **Title page** — you arrive at the name and the promise: a room built for listening.  
2. **The door** — the threshold from street noise to a quieter contract.  
3. **The room** — low light, wood, speakers placed like instruments.  
4. **The pour** — cocktails measured with the same care as a side A.  
5. **The record** — one album, played through, no skipping.  
6. **The stay** — how to find us and reserve a seat; the night holds.

## 3. The energy curve
- **Title page:** hushed anticipation (calm).  
- **The door:** a held breath at the threshold (quiet tension).  
- **The room:** enveloping warmth opening out (awe/intimacy).  
- **The pour:** tactile focus and appetite (delight).  
- **The record:** deep, suspended attention (reverence/calm intensity).  
- **The stay:** settled resolve, an invitation accepted (resolve/calm).

## 4. How should someone feel while scrolling, stage by stage, and what is the ONE moment they should remember?
**Stage by stage:** arrival → threshold hush → immersion in the room → sensory pleasure of the pour → reverent listening → quiet commitment to come.  
**The ONE moment they should remember:** the room chapter where the lights drop in feeling and the copy insists the album is played all the way through — listening as a full-body promise.  
**Peak (sentence a visitor would say to a friend):** “It’s the site where the room goes quiet and you realize they actually make you listen to the whole record.”  
**Peak lives in:** Chapter 5, “The record”.

## 5. One thing this site should do that no site they have seen does
A **folio equalizer** lives in the margin for the whole page: a slim vertical column of bars that reacts to scroll velocity (livelier when you move, settling when you pause) and stamps a permanent groove mark at each chapter you pass. By the colophon it reads as a trace of your listening journey down the page — navigation and memory in one bespoke instrument.

## 6. How far from premium-minimal they want to go
**Editorial** with cinematic warmth — closer to a printed feature/program than a glossy nightclub flyer. Restrained, paper-and-shadow, but rich in atmosphere (amber, grain, deep blacks, photographic inserts).

## 7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?
**Distinct scenes/chapters.** The evening is experienced as discrete movements (threshold, room, pour, record, stay), separated by hard chapter cuts and intertitles, like a program or film chapters.

## 8. What assets do they already have?
**Nothing provided.** No logo files, no photography, no footage, no brand kit. I will generate moody photographic stills for chapter media columns (via KIE AI if available) and otherwise build with authored CSS/SVG atmosphere; any generated assets are clearly treated as created for this fictional concept.

## Feeling curve (written before acts; devices secondary)
1. **Anticipation** — title page: large typographic name on dark paper ground, folio awake, no image yet.  
2. **Threshold hush** — The door: intertitle cut to near-black ground, a narrow column of text about crossing inside.  
3. **Immersion** — The room: hard cut to warm ground, photographic room image in media column, copy about placement and light.  
4. **Delight/appetite** — The pour: cooler amber ground, drink/counter detail image, precise measured copy.  
5. **Reverence (PEAK)** — The record: deepest ground, sleeve/turntable detail, largest chapter span and silence before the insistence “played through.”  
6. **Resolve** — The stay: return toward paper-black, colophon-style close with reserve line as running text, folio trace complete.

Adjacent feelings differ at each step; peak (5) has the most room and follows the tactile focus of (4).

## The peak
- **Visitor sentence:** “It’s the site where the room goes quiet and you realize they actually make you listen to the whole record.”  
- **Act/chapter:** 5, “The record”.  
- **Treatment:** longest chapter, strongest visual stillness, copy pared back to one insistence; folio equalizer settles as if holding a note.

## Tell-someone sentence (completed, experience not device)
“It’s the site where you scroll down an evening in a listening bar and the margin keeps a living trace of the record you’re moving through until you decide to stay for the whole thing.”

## Authored silence
- The space after the Chapter 5 intertitle before the body copy appears is intentional (a held beat of quiet).  
- The colophon close is intentionally quiet/small — resolution, not a trailing fade.
