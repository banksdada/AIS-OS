# What kinds of apps and systems we build

Use this when a visitor asks "what else can you do", "what types of apps do you
build", or wants to know whether their idea is something we handle. Give two or
three categories that fit their situation, with one real example each.

## Business and operations apps

- Care management platforms for UK care providers: residents, care plans,
  staff, inspections, CQC-friendly records. Example: AIcare.
- Job management and scheduling SaaS: customers, jobs, conflict-free slots,
  feature flags. Example: WebAwesome Scheduler.
- Rota and shift planning. Example: RotaMate.
- CRM and client trackers on Google Sheets or a database, with email follow-ups.
- Invoicing, quoting and proposal tools.

## AI apps and automation

- Website assistants and chatbots grounded in a company's own content, with live
  booking and lead capture. Example: Webby, the assistant on this site.
- AI readiness audits and scored reports with PDF output.
  Example: https://ai-audit.webawesome.co.uk
- Document and CV intelligence: parsing, scoring and matching.
  Example: https://github.com/banksdada/flux-job-intelligence
- Workflow automation between tools (Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Stripe,
  WhatsApp, webhooks) so manual admin stops.

## Websites and e-commerce

- Business, charity and church sites with content management, donations,
  events, sermons, member areas and newsletters.
  Examples: https://gracechurchlondon.org, https://gpchapel.org,
  https://africanculturenetwork.org
- E-commerce and telemedicine platforms in Nigeria and the UK.
  Example: https://medibox.ng
- Booking and appointment sites for clinics and service businesses.
- Logistics and fleet-facing sites with tracking pages.
  Example: https://ypopularglobal.co.uk
- Portfolio and publishing sites. Example: https://kingsleybooks.online

## Mobile and multi-tenant products

- Mobile apps built with nativePHP on Laravel (no rewrite in another language).
- Multi-tenant SaaS where each client gets its own scoped data and branding.
- Subscription billing with Stripe and customer self-service portals.

## How to answer "what can you do for me"

- Ask what the visitor's business does and where the manual work sits, then name
  one or two categories above that fit, with the matching example.
- Offer a free 15-minute discovery call to scope it, or the AI readiness audit if
  they are still exploring AI.
- Do not promise custom prices or timelines in chat; those come from the call.
