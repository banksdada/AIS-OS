# AI services

AI is a core part of what WebAwesome does, not an add-on. We use AI in our own
delivery and we build AI systems for clients.

## AI readiness audit

- A structured audit of how a business actually uses AI today, scored against a
  Crawl / Walk / Run maturity model.
- Based on a 12-question questionnaire plus public evidence, delivered as a
  one-page A4 summary and a full report with PDF export.
- The tool itself is live at https://ai-audit.webawesome.co.uk and is built and
  run by WebAwesome.
- Start with a free 15-minute discovery call; the audit scope and fee are agreed
  after that call.

## AI Operating System (AIOS) setup for clients

- We install and tailor a personal AI operating system for a founder or small
  team: one workspace that knows the business and can act on it.
- Includes onboarding of the business context, connections to the tools the
  client already uses (email, calendar, CRM, documents), and a set of reusable
  skills and agents for recurring work.
- Practical output: daily briefs, follow-up chasing, proposal and audit drafts,
  lead research, and automations that replace manual copy-paste work.
- Delivered hands-on with the client, with rules for what the AI may and may not
  do on its own (drafts, never sends).

## AI automation and workflow work

- Automating repetitive business processes: lead capture and routing, invoice
  and follow-up reminders, reporting, content preparation, data tidy-up.
- Built with plain APIs, schedulers and scripts that the client can own, rather
  than locked into one vendor.

## Custom AI agents and chat assistants

- Website chat assistants grounded in a client's own content, with lead capture,
  live booking and human handoff. Webby, the assistant on this site, is one we
  built for ourselves.
- Internal assistants that answer from company documents and draft replies.

## AI product builds

- End-to-end AI product work: discovery, prototype, build and deploy on our own
  infrastructure, including model integration, evaluation and guard-rails.

## AI training and mentoring

- Through mentoringba we teach business analysts and teams how to use AI in
  analysis work: prompting, automation, and the mindset, method and machine
  model behind real adoption.
