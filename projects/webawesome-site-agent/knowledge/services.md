# WebAwesome services

WebAwesome is a UK digital agency (webawesome.co.uk). We help small businesses,
startups, charities and SMEs turn business challenges into digital products that
work.

## Website design and build

- Bespoke, mobile-responsive websites on a CMS (WordPress) so clients can edit
  their own content.
- Built for startups, sole traders, charities and SMEs on realistic budgets.
- Includes search engine friendly structure and a customer-centred layout.
- Typical build takes a few weeks depending on scope; a blog setup package is
  quoted on the site at £170 and covers theme installation, WordPress setup and
  essential plugins over 2 to 5 days.

## Hosting

- Website hosting included free for the first year with a website build.
- SSL certificates, email accounts and subdomain support through a UK-based
  hosting partner.

## SEO

- SEO packages and technical SEO work: keyword and metadata work, site
  structure, local SEO for small businesses, content guidance and ongoing
  improvements.

## Web care and maintenance (WordPress)

- Monitoring and protection against spam, malware and hacking, with weekly
  scanning.
- Backups every 3 days with restore when something goes wrong.
- No long-term contracts, no setup or termination fees.

## Digital consulting and product engineering

- Strategy, product, AI, engineering and growth as one team across the product
  lifecycle.
- Business analysis, experience design and engineering for web apps and SaaS
  products (Laravel, Python/FastAPI, Next.js, Postgres).
- Discovery calls are free, 15 minutes, and are the practical first step: we
  discuss the challenge, what success looks like and the options worth
  exploring.

## Careers and business analysis training

Through mentoringba (an arm of WebAwesome) we coach people into business
analysis careers: mentoring, practical exercises and community support.

## What we are not

- We do not publish fixed prices for custom builds, SEO retainers or consulting;
  these are quoted after a discovery call.
- We do not promise rankings, traffic or revenue figures.
