Growth Tips For Your Business Growing your business or organisation is a necessary strategy everyone should consider. Find below how to grow your customer base which is important to your business success. Expect challenges too Here are some practical growth tips to help you grow your customer base. Define and know your customers Understanding your customer archetype/persona needs and developing customer-centric products and services that meet their needs is vital to customer retention. You can gain insight into your customers by customizing your product/services and encouraging them to provide you with continuous feedback. This will drive engagement and growth for both parties Offer Exceptional Service/Product Everyone appreciates a business that does more than is expected. Never hesitate to go the extra mile in delivering great products and services your customers will be glad to come back for. For example, sending a birthday greeting every year is awesome, affordable and will win the heart of your customers. Your customers will not only remember great service they will also be more likely to refer other people to you. Nurture existing customers and devise new opportunities Create a simple structure to keep exciting and updating existing customers through blog updates, newsletters, social media updates, and more. At the same time, look for opportunities to get more work and build your customer base. Make sure you find the right balance between nurturing customers and finding new ones. It will be worth your while! Social media activities The world has gone social. People tweet cursed and moan and complain on any platform they can. However, humans also give complements on these social media platforms. Social media provides an opportunitty for your business to listen to your customer base and gain insight into potential opportunities. Through social listening you can find out what customers are saying about you, gain insight into their behaviour, identify keywords and trends that appeal to your target market and so improve your customer service. Social media can help you to build your business profile and attract new customers to help grow your business or organisation Networking events Don’t underestimate the power of networking events locally, or nationally. These drive potential opportunities for collaborations and business growth Attending or organising networking allows you to build relationships with other people and encourage them to refer customers to you through word of mouth. Create and host/sponsor events Hosting, sponsoring or creating your own event can be a great way to get to know your customers and build relationships. Invite some of your best existing customers and encourage them to bring their friends. Through this you can create potential new customer base to help move your business forward Support your community Building brand awareness in your local community is a great way to attract new business. Consider a sponsorship or participating in a community event to raise your business awareness. You can participate in meetups, sponsor the less privileged, offer discounted services, and more.
