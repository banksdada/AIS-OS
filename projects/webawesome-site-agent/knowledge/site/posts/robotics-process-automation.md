Robotic process automation is the future of process optimisation and automation Robotic process automation (or RPA is an emerging form of business process automation technology based on the notion of software robots or artificial intelligence (AI) workers. It is the future of work. Robotic process automation is the use of software with artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning capabilities to handle high-volume, repeatable tasks that previously required humans to perform. These tasks can include queries, calculations, and maintenance of records, sending email notifications, uploading and downloading documents and transactions. Thoughtonomy defined it as the innovative use of software to perform repetitive, rules-based knowledge work across an organization as a substitute for, or aide to, human workers.
