Digital Transformation is the new buzzword What does Digital Transformation Mean? Wikipedia – Digital Transformation is not necessarily about digital technology, but about the fact that technology, which is digital, allows people to solve their traditional problems. And they prefer this digital solution to the old solution. Scoop – Digital transformation is the profound transformation of business and organizational activities, processes, competencies, and models to fully leverage the changes and opportunities of a mix of digital technologies and their accelerating impact across society in a strategic and prioritized way, with present and future shifts in mind. Itpro – Digital transformation is more than just a buzzword – it’s a process of using technology to radically change your business. There is no one definition of digital transformation as a word. It is a buzzword that is taking over business both local and international. One thing is true though, Businesses need to respond and solve customer problems quickly, efficiently, affordably and effectively!! Digital transformation is enabling your business to solve problems, provide goods and services that delight and exceed customer expectations. Any transformation that does not add value to the end user is a walk in the park. It is beyond spending millions on new tech. Its a combination of people, processes, and technology usually with people at the forefront of every initiative. Customers want better services, affordable goods and products. They want businesses that deliver on their promises and possibly exceed their expectations. Transformation initiatives like Robotics Process Automation – RPA, Mobile Application, Customer Relationship Management and a lot more are initiated to drive more value to the end users. Taking a high-level overview of Robotics Process Automation, it offers such benefits as Increasing business and process efficiency Providing 100% Accuracy 24/7 Productivity Improving Customer Experience Enhancing Visibility Improving Time to Market Driving Compliance with Regulations Enabling Faster business evolution Reducing Costs Speeding up process time Boosting Scalability Optimizing Resources Our Opinion on the hype All Digital Transformation initiatives should drive customer engagement, improve service delivery and products design. It should improve SLA delivery for companies that deliver business services. It should improve customer perception of your business. There are no digital transformation benefits without these fundamental benefits . Al l these benefits drive customer engagement and business profitability. A lot of businesses are engaging in a digital transformation to drive improvements in customer services, automating tasks and updating processes, compliance with regulations and many more. The benefits are continuous and rely on human engagement to optimally drive home the objectives of the initiative, which is to improve customer value delivery.
