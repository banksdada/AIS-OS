A Bad website just doesnt cut it! A lot of good businesses lose money because their websites are not meeting up, which leads to bad customer experience and eventual loss of customers. Imagine how many people visit your nice website, see some error or irregularity, and drop out never to return again to your website. The average user never returns to a sub-par website and will immediately move on to a competitor website that delivers the value that they need from the website. The power has somewhat moved to the consumer who is inundated with choices nowadays Are You Losing Customers Due To Your Website? In today’s evolving technology-driven world your customer-facing website needs to become the main resource for providing much-needed information to your users. It should provide guidance, information and eventual call to action encouraging your users to prod more by repeat visits or eventual sales. Your website should make things easy for your end users The mistakes listed below are some of the things that can make your website drive potential business away In today’s technology-driven world your website needs to become the main resource for reaching out to your potential customers. Your website should inform your customers about what you do and should show them why they should choose you over your competitors. Your website should be a key part of your business and it is important to make sure your website isn’t turning away potential business. The following 5 mistakes are things your website may potentially be doing, which can drive away business. Poor Outlay, content, and Design If your website is poorly designed or outlined, too busy, contains typos, grammatical errors, or simply rabid looking you may displace potential customers from being interested in working with your business. It is usually advisable to do your keyword research, plan your WordPress outline design your content and features, and try, retry, and retry. This should be evolving No defined Purpose A potential customer should have an understanding of the purpose of your website from the first 10seconds of visiting your website. If this isn’t visible, customers lose interest and seek other websites that have a better-defined purpose than yours. Links Don’t Open in a New Tab If you are linking to another website, you should have it open in a new tab. If you don’t do this, you are making your potential customers work to get back to your webpage instead of them just need to head back to a tab they already have open. The easier you make it for your potential customers to stay on your website the better. It is important to go to your website and click on all links (including social media links) to make sure they open in a new tab. Being Responsive Your website will be viewed 75% or more of the time from a mobile or tablet or some other mobile device. If your website isn’t responsive, customers will lose interest and move to a responsive website. Also, your website should load within 3 seconds as recommended by google . Search Engine Optimisation is what will boost your search engine results. It also improves your search and visibility. Using keywords in your site content will make it easier for the customer to find you when searching on Google, Bing, Yandex or some other search engine You can also make it easy for the customer to contact you by providing all your relevant contact information that can help your customer contact you further. Try including live chat options as this is the quickest way for the customer to make inquiries and get responses.
