# Some Ways to Find New Blog Ideas

Source: https://www.webawesome.co.uk/some-easy-ways-to-find-fresh-blog-ideas-topics/
Updated: 2024-12-27T22:45:37

Blogging is a great way of speaking to your audience, be that existing customers or new ones. Reality is that often you would run out of blog ideas and sometimes it can be difficult to keep things flowing. Below are some gold tips for coming up with new blog content ideas. Below are some ways to find new blog ideas Track how people are finding your website People will find your website in so many ways. And the most popular ways will be via a search engine of sorts. With a software like Google Analytics you can view which keywords visitors are using to find your website. These keywords can then be dropped into blog titles, along with the content itself. This simple approach makes your content more relevant and more likely to be ranked by search engines. Ask your customers for continuous feedback Your very own customers are a great resource for coming up with new content. Drop them a friendly email, pick up the phone, or send a DM on social. Ask them all sorts of questions, build engagement and if possible create an avenue or social community where they can complain, compliment and interact with you. Gathering feedback like this will spark new ideas, and enables you to cover topics that like-minded people might enjoy. Hire Writers for ideas Another alternative could be to outsource some or all of the writing. Services like Upwork and Freelancer allow you to tap into a bustling community of writers. Just set yourself a budget, shortlist some freelancers, and get in touch. Be ready to give them an introduction to your business, and most importantly, make sure you draw up a solid brief. Be as specific as you can, detailing what you want, what you don’t want, and some examples of content you like. Why Webawesome?
