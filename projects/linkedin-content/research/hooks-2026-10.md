# Research — proven LinkedIn hooks (verified 2026-10-21)

**AIOS rule:** every hook carries its URL and the stat that made it viral. No URL, no hook.

Source of record: **Linqin** LinkedIn hook library — https://linqin.ai/linkedin-hooks — built from a corpus of **8,300 viral LinkedIn posts**, engagement re-verified 2026-10-20. This is a third-party aggregator, not LinkedIn itself, so treat the numbers as directional evidence, not gospel.

## The headline finding

The **Story Open** is the strongest pattern found, and it is the one our current bank does not use.

| Pattern | Avg reactions | vs corpus avg (282) |
|---|---|---|
| Story Open (AI/ML founders) | 460 | 1.6x |
| Story Open (B2B SaaS / data) | 434 | 1.5x |

A Story Open "starts inside a moment. A founder hears a sharp warning. A team launches something." The reader must want the next line.

## The exact mistake our bank makes

Verbatim from the source, on B2B SaaS/data hooks:

> "The common mistake is starting too wide. 'Data is changing fast' is easy to skip."

Our Day 1 opens: *"Everyone is selling AI."* That is a claim, not a moment. It starts wide. Same for most of the bank.

## Real hooks from the corpus (structure, not words)

These are other people's posts. **Copy the structure, never the words.**

| Hook (abbreviated) | Author | Reactions | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| "An Anthropic engineer just told us not to build agents..." | Basia Kubicka | 2k | Named insider + blunt warning |
| "Last month, we brought Copilot Cowork to the Frontier program..." | Charles Lamanna | 1.9k | Recent concrete event + result |
| "Speaking at the AI Impact Summit among... brilliant minds..." | Ananya Birla | 1.6k | Live event, then the shift it revealed |
| "294 days. The longest break I've ever taken..." | Erica Volini | n/a | Specific number + personal stake |

**Patterns distilled:**
1. **Insider + warning** — "X just told us [surprising thing]"
2. **Concrete recent event** — a date, a launch, a client, then what happened
3. **Specific number first** — "294 days", "£3,400", "47 emails"
4. **Scene before claim** — put the reader inside the moment; the lesson arrives after

## What this means for our bank

The 30 drafts are credible but built to inform, not to stop a scroll. To have a chance of performing they need **new openings** in the Story Open shape — which means we need real moments from Banks. That is exactly what `brain/` is for, and why the interview comes before the rewrite.

## Caveats (read before trusting any number)

- Third-party aggregator; the 8,300-post corpus and its averages are their claim, not independently verified.
- "Reactions" is not reach, and neither is shown to be causal. A pattern that correlates with reactions is not proven to *cause* them.
- The corpus skews to AI/founder/tech voices with large existing audiences. A hook that worked for someone with 2M followers may do little for a smaller account — audience size is a confound the source does not control for.
- **No hook guarantees virality.** This is a system for improving odds, not a lever that produces a viral post on demand.
