POSITION SUMMARY 
The Senior Business Analyst will play a pivotal role in two interconnected strategic 
transformation initiatives: the replacement of Uniworld’s legacy Longitude reservations 
platform and the development of Trafalgar River’s booking, servicing and operational 
capabilities. Acting as the bridge between business stakeholders and implementation teams, 
they will independently lead the analysis required to define needs, shape practical solutions and 
deliver measurable business outcomes. 
Sitting within the TTC Tech PMO and reporting to the Head of PMO, the role will work across 
reservations, sales and customer servicing, digital commerce, CRM, cruise operations, finance, 
data and technology. It will coordinate analysis across multiple brands, markets, platforms, 
suppliers and stakeholder groups, with Longitude Replacement as the primary focus and 
Trafalgar River as a closely related project. 
 
Operating across the full delivery lifecycle, the Senior Business Analyst will lead discovery, 
requirements elicitation and validation, current- and future-state process mapping, gap and

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 
• Serve as the primary bridge between business stakeholders and technology 
implementation teams for assigned Longitude Replacement and Trafalgar River 
workstreams, building trusted relationships and maintaining clear communication 
throughout delivery 
• Lead end-to-end business analysis activities across the full delivery lifecycle, from 
discovery and options assessment through requirements validation, design, build, 
testing, implementation and benefits realisation 
• Plan and facilitate workshops, interviews and working sessions; map current- and future-
state processes; and analyse manual workarounds, exceptions, operational risks and 
opportunities for simplification or automation 
• Elicit, document, analyse and validate end-to-end business needs, translating them into 
clear functional and non-functional requirements, business rules, use cases, user stories, 
process models and measurable acceptance criteria 
• Identify requirements gaps, overlaps, conflicts and dependencies; support prioritisation 
and impact assessment; and maintain traceability from agreed business objectives 
through solution design, build, testing and implementation 
• Collaborate with product owners, solution architects, engineering teams and vendors on 
solution design, fit-gap assessment and business validation, documenting interfaces, 
data flows and cross-system requirements without owning the technical architecture 
• Assess impacts across reservations, digital, CRM, payments, customer servicing, cruise 
operations, data, reporting and third-party platforms, ensuring decisions for Longitude 
Replacement appropriately consider Trafalgar River requirements and wider TTC 
dependencies 
• Coordinate business analysis inputs to UAT by defining scenarios and acceptance 
criteria, supporting appropriate coverage and business-user participation, and providing 
requirements clarification and functional defect triage; contribute to migration, cutover, 
readiness and operational transition activities as required 
• Act as the central coordination point for business analysis across assigned workstreams, 
maintaining the quality and consistency of deliverables, escalating requirement-related 
risks and dependencies, facilitating timely decisions and aligning analysis activity with 
programme plans and milestones 
The above list is not exhaustive, and you may be required to carry out any other reasonable 
task as requested, appropriate to the scope of the post.

ROLE SUCCESS CRITERIA 
• Business objectives, operational problems, requirements, assumptions and decisions are 
clearly defined, prioritised, validated and traceable 
• Current and future processes are understood end to end, including exceptions, manual 
workarounds, business rules and impacts on customers, colleagues and operations 
• Dependencies across Longitude Replacement, Trafalgar River and connected platforms 
are identified early, assessed consistently and communicated to the appropriate 
accountable roles 
• Proposed solutions are objectively assessed against agreed business needs, with gaps, 
constraints and business implications made visible before decisions are taken 
• UAT, migration, cutover and business-readiness activities are supported by clear 
requirements, acceptance criteria and timely analysis throughout delivery 
• Implemented capabilities are operationally viable, support measurable business benefits 
and remain aligned to the agreed scope and customer and commercial outcomes 
KEY INDIVIDUAL TRAITS 
• Structured, commercially aware and evidence-led, with excellent analytical judgement 
and attention to detail 
• A clear and persuasive communicator who can translate complex technology, 
integration, process and data topics into understandable business implications 
• Credible and collaborative, with the confidence to influence senior stakeholders, 
challenge assumptions constructively and resolve conflicting requirements 
• Comfortable navigating ambiguity, organisational complexity, competing priorities and 
change across a global, matrixed environment 
• Outcome-focused, balancing analytical rigour with customer, operational and 
commercial awareness and pragmatic delivery decisions 
• Proactive and resourceful, able to uncover root causes, anticipate delivery risks and 
identify opportunities for process improvement and automation 
• Hands-on and self-directed, able to lead complex cross-functional analysis while 
respecting the accountabilities of programme, product, architecture and test leadership 
 
YOUR EXPERIENCE AND EXPERTISE  
• At least 7 years’ experience as a Business Analyst, including substantial experience 
independently leading analysis on complex enterprise technology and business 
transformation programmes 
• Proven experience of enterprise system implementation, replacement, modernisation or 
integration, ideally involving business-critical legacy platforms and significant 
operational change 
• Expertise in requirements elicitation, documentation, analysis and validation, with strong 
current- and future-state process mapping, gap analysis, business-rule definition and 
requirements traceability 
• Strong technical literacy and experience analysing solutions involving multiple systems, 
APIs, integrations, data flows, third-party platforms and complex end-to-end workflows 
• Experience supporting functional solution design, supplier fit-gap assessment and 
business validation, with the ability to translate architectural choices and technology 
constraints into clear business impacts 
• Strong stakeholder management and workshop facilitation skills, including influencing 
senior leaders, coordinating cross-functional analysis and resolving ambiguous or 
conflicting requirements
