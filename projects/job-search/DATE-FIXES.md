# CV date corrections — apply to the Drive originals

The AIOS has read-only access to the Drive `resumes` folder, so it cannot edit
the original DOCX/PDF files. Make these edits by hand in the source files.

Corrected copies (text only) are in this folder for reference.

## 1. Webawesome start date — April 2022, not April 2024

Three variants say April 2024. All three should say **April 2022**.

- `BA_CV-CMS_ .docx` — "Product Delivery Analyst Consultant | Webawesome Consult UK"
- `BA_CV_legacyR.docx` — "Webawesome Consult UK, London/Aetotech Consult"
- `BA_CV_util (1).docx` — "Webawesome Consult UK/Aetotech Limited, London"

`BA_CV-HMRC.docx` and `BA_CV_SFCRM.docx.pdf` already say April 2022. No change.

## 2. NHS Professionals dates — in BA_CV_SFCRM.docx.pdf

The SFCRM variant lists NHS Professionals as **Dec 2018 – May 2019**. Those are
NHSBSA's dates. NHS Professionals is **Dec 2020 – Feb 2021** (correct in the
other four variants). SFCRM has this wrong.

## 3. "8+ years" in summaries — understates the history

`BA_CV-HMRC.docx` and `BA_CV_SFCRM.docx.pdf` both open with "8+ years". The
history runs from 2013 (First Bank) or 2016 (Laser Loans). Either is more than
8 years. Consider "10+ years" or "over a decade", and make sure the same claim
is consistent across variants.

## Why this matters

Contract and permanent recruiters check dates against references. Three
variants giving three different start dates for the same employer is the kind
of thing that costs an interview.
