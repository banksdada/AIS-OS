# Overcomers guest FAQ — Cutlass Court

Extracted from overcomers-faq-cutlass-court.html (source of truth).

## What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00pm and check-out is by 11:00am.

## How does check-in work?

It's self check-in. You'll find your keys in a key safe at the property, so there's no need to meet anyone. We send your check-in instructions on the day you arrive.

## Can I arrive late?

Yes, there's no time limit for late arrivals.

## Can I drop my bags off early?

Yes, if the apartment is empty. If it isn't, we can't take your bags early.

## Is parking free?

Yes, parking is free.

## Do I need a parking permit?

No. Cutlass Court has its own car park under the building, so you don't need a permit.

## How many cars can I bring?

Two. Two bays are reserved for your apartment.

## How do I get into the car park?

Use the fob attached to your key bunch.

## Is the car park indoors?

Yes, it's indoors, under the building. Tall or large vehicles may not fit, so please message us before you travel if you're unsure.

## Is the car park monitored?

Yes, the car park is monitored.

## Can I charge an electric car?

Sorry, there's no electric vehicle charging.

## What if someone is parked in my bay?

Give us a call and we'll report it to the building manager to sort out.

## Where can a third car park?

There's on-street parking right in front of the building.

## Which floor is the apartment on?

Cutlass Court is on the 5th floor.

## Is there step-free access?

No, there are steps from the street to the front door.

## Which station is nearest?

Birmingham New Street is the nearest station.

## How far is it to the Utilita Arena, Sea Life Centre and Brindleyplace?

It's about a 10-minute walk.

## Where do taxis pick up and drop off?

Right in front of the building.

## How many guests can it sleep?

The maximum is four guests, in two bedrooms.

## What beds are there?

Both bedrooms have double beds.

## Is there a sofa bed?

Yes, there's a sofa bed, but it doesn't change the maximum of four guests.

## Do the bedrooms have doors and storage?

Yes. Each bedroom has a door that closes and its own wardrobe.

## What are the bathrooms like?

There are two shower rooms.

## Are bed linen, towels and toiletries provided?

Yes. Bed linen, towels and toiletries are all provided.

## Can I get fresh linen during a longer stay?

Yes, we can change it on request. This is charged separately.

## Do you have a cot or high chair?

Yes, one is available for families. Just ask us.

## Is the balcony furnished?

The balcony has no furniture of its own, but you're welcome to carry a table and chairs out from the apartment.

## Can I use the balcony at night, or smoke there?

Please don't use the balcony at night, as neighbours live close by. Smoking isn't allowed on the balcony.

## Is the balcony safe for young children?

Yes, with caution. Please keep a close eye on young children.

## What's in the kitchen?

A fully equipped kitchen, with cooking items, plates, cutlery and glasses for your whole group.

## Is there a washing machine?

Yes, there's a washing machine.

## Are tea, coffee and milk provided?

Yes, you'll find welcome supplies, including tea and coffee.

## Is there heating and air conditioning?

Yes, both. You can control them yourself.

## Is there a hairdryer, iron and ironing board?

Yes, all three.

## Is there a TV?

Yes, with Netflix.

## Is there a dining table?

Yes, it seats four.

## How fast is the Wi-Fi?

Fast Wi-Fi is included. When we tested it, speeds were 350 to 500 Mbps.

## Is there a desk to work at?

Yes, there's a desk and a suitable chair.

## Will I hear noise from outside?

You may hear noise from events in the area on busy days. If a quiet stay matters to you, it's worth checking what's on nearby before you book.

## Are there neighbours?

Yes, other residents live in the building, so please keep noise down at night.

## What are the quiet hours?

Quiet hours are in the afternoons.

## Is there building work nearby?

Nothing is under construction nearby that we know of.

## Are there smoke and carbon monoxide alarms?

Yes. There are smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms and a first aid kit.

## Is the building secure?

Yes, the building has an intercom, and the car park opens with a fob.

## Are there cameras?

Yes, at the door into each apartment.

## How long can I stay?

There's no minimum or maximum length of stay.

## Is there a cleaning fee?

Yes. The cleaning fee is paid separately when you book.

## Do you take a deposit?

No, we don't take a deposit.

## How can I pay?

By card, either through Booking.com or directly on our website.

## Does the price change with the number of guests?

Yes, the price changes with the number of guests.

## Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Book direct for our best rates, and ask us about discounts for longer stays.

## What is your cancellation policy?

If you don't take up your stay at all, you'll receive a full refund.

## Can I change my dates?

Yes, as long as the new dates are free. There is no fee.

## What if the apartment isn't available when I arrive?

We'll offer you an alternative or a refund.

## Are pets allowed?

Sorry, we can't allow pets.

## Can I smoke or vape?

No smoking or vaping anywhere in the property.

## Are parties or events allowed?

No parties or events, please.

## Can I have visitors?

Yes, as long as you tell us beforehand. If a visitor will stay overnight, please let us know in advance, as extra costs may apply.

## Is there a minimum age to book?

The lead guest must be 18 or over.

## Do I need to show ID at check-in?

No, we don't ask for ID at check-in.

## What if something is damaged or a key is lost?

You're responsible for any damage or lost keys, and we'll ask you to cover the cost.
